# Third-party materials

PlanSpace is evaluated on task definitions derived from BEHAVIOR-1K/BDDL and on
outputs produced by publicly available language-model checkpoints. This public
artifact does not redistribute model weights or simulator assets.

Users who obtain upstream materials separately must comply with the upstream
licenses and terms for:

- BEHAVIOR-1K and BDDL task definitions;
- Qwen3 model checkpoints;
- Microsoft Phi-4-mini-instruct;
- Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3; and
- AllenAI OLMo-2-1124-7B-Instruct.

The frozen configuration records the exact public checkpoint identifiers and
immutable revisions used for the primary experiment.
